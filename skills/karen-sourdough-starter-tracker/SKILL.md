# Karen Sourdough Starter Tracker

## What It Does

Keeps a simple feeding log for a fictional sourdough starter. It records when the starter was last fed, how much flour and water went in, and how high it rose, then estimates when the next feeding is due and whether the starter looks ready for baking.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when a home baker is keeping a starter alive and wants a tidy record instead of scattered sticky notes. It is most helpful before planning a bake, after missing a feeding, or when deciding whether a sluggish starter needs a few more cycles.

## Inputs

- The date and time of the most recent feeding.
- The feeding ratio, such as 1:1:1 or 1:2:2.
- Flour type, such as all-purpose, whole wheat, or rye.
- Approximate room temperature.
- Optional notes on rise height, smell, or bubble activity.

## Output

A short feeding log table, an estimated next feeding time, and a one-line readiness verdict such as "ready to bake" or "feed twice more before baking."

## Example Prompt

```text
My fictional starter was fed yesterday at 8am with a 1:1:1 ratio of rye flour,
the kitchen sits around 70F, and it doubled in about six hours.
When should I feed it next and is it ready to bake?
```

## Safety Notes

Use only invented baking details supplied in the prompt. Do not pull from personal files, personal history, private notes, company information, customer data, credentials, or real workplace documents. Timing estimates are rough guidance for a practice exercise, not food-safety advice.
