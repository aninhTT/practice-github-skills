# Picnic Menu Planner

## What It Does

Turns a made-up picnic plan into a simple menu and packing list. It balances easy finger foods, a main dish, snacks, and drinks for the group size, flags which items travel well in warm weather, and groups everything into a checklist so nothing gets left on the kitchen counter.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when you are planning a fictional picnic for 2 to 20 people and want a quick menu that is easy to carry and needs no cooking on site. It helps most when the group has a mix of tastes, such as a couple of vegetarians or someone who doesn't like spicy food, or when the picnic spot has no shade or tables.

## Inputs

- The number of invented guests and the picnic length (a quick lunch or an all-afternoon hangout).
- The made-up setting, such as a park lawn, a beach, or a lakeside trail.
- The expected weather (hot, mild, or chilly).
- Simple food preferences, such as "two vegetarians" or "one guest dislikes spicy food".
- Optional: a budget level (low, medium, or treat-yourself) and whether there's a cooler.

## Output

- A short menu split into Mains, Sides & Snacks, Sweets, and Drinks, with rough amounts for the group.
- A note next to any item that should stay cold or might not hold up in the heat.
- A packing checklist grouped by Food, Gear (blanket, utensils, napkins), and Clean-up (trash bags, wipes).
- One make-ahead tip for the night before.

## Example Prompt

```text
I'm planning a made-up picnic for 6 friends at a sunny park lawn on a hot
afternoon. Two guests are vegetarian, and Tobin doesn't like spicy food.
We have one small cooler and a medium budget. Give me a menu with amounts,
flag anything that won't do well in the heat, and make a packing checklist.
```

## Safety Notes

This is a fictional practice skill for learning GitHub. Use invented guest names and made-up details only. Do not include real people's names, personal information, company or customer data, internal workflows, credentials, or anything copied from a real document. The skill gives general menu ideas and does not know about real allergies or medical dietary needs, so always check food safety and allergies with your actual guests.
