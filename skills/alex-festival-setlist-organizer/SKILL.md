# Alex Festival Setlist Organizer

## What It Does

Turns a fictional music festival lineup into a personal, conflict-free schedule. It lays out which act to catch on which stage at what time, flags overlapping sets so you can decide who to prioritize, and leaves buffer time to walk between stages.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when a learner wants to practice describing a simple scheduling workflow with invented event data, such as planning an imaginary weekend at a made-up music festival.

## Inputs

- A fictional festival name and the days it runs.
- A list of made-up acts with their stage and set times.
- The walking time between stages (a rough guess is fine).
- Any acts the learner does not want to miss.

## Output

A day-by-day schedule that lists each chosen act with its stage and time, marks every set conflict, suggests which act to keep when two overlap, and adds walking buffers between stages.

## Example Prompt

```text
Here is a fake two-day festival lineup for "Sunset Canyon Fest" with invented bands and stage times. Build me a personal schedule, flag the conflicts, and tell me who to prioritize.
```

## Safety Notes

Use invented festivals, bands, and schedules only. Do not include real ticket information, personal location history, payment details, private contacts, or any real event data tied to a specific person.
