# Ashley Trivia Night Scorekeeper

## What It Does

Tallies scores for a fictional pub trivia night. Takes made-up team names and their per-round points, adds up each team's total, and produces a tidy scoreboard ranked from first to last place.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when practicing how to organize and total round-by-round scores for a pretend trivia event, without relying on any real people, venues, or private data.

## Inputs

- A fictional trivia night name.
- A short list of made-up team names.
- Points each team earned per round (any number of rounds).

## Output

A ranked scoreboard showing each team, their per-round points, and their grand total, with the winning team listed first.

## Example Prompt

```text
Score a fictional trivia night called "Tuesday Brain Bash" with three teams — Quiz Khalifa, The Wikipedians, and Nacho Average Team — across three rounds, and tell me who won.
```

## Safety Notes

Do not use real people's names, real venues, actual event results, private group chats, or any personal or company information. Keep every team, score, and event detail invented.
