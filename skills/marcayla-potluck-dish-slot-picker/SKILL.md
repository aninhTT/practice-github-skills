# Marcayla Potluck Dish Slot Picker

## What It Does

Suggests what dish someone should bring to a fictional potluck so the spread
stays balanced. It looks at which slots are already claimed (appetizers, mains,
sides, desserts, drinks) and recommends a category that is under-filled, plus a
couple of made-up dish ideas that fit it.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when you want to practice describing a simple "fill the gap"
planning workflow for a make-believe gathering, without relying on any real
guest list or private event details.

## Inputs

- A fictional potluck theme or occasion.
- The number of imaginary guests.
- A list of dishes already claimed and their categories.
- Any dietary notes you want to invent (for example, "one vegetarian slot").

## Output

- One recommended dish category to bring next.
- Two or three fictional dish ideas for that category.
- A short balance summary showing which slots are still open.

## Example Prompt

```text
I'm planning a fake summer-picnic potluck for 8 pretend guests. So far people
have claimed two desserts and one main. Tell me what category to bring next and
suggest a few dish ideas.
```

## Safety Notes

Use invented potlucks and imaginary guests only. Do not include real guest
lists, contact information, addresses, personal calendars, or any private or
company event details.
