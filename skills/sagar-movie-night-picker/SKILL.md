# Movie Night Picker

## What It Does

Suggests a short list of movies for a fictional movie night, using the group's preferred genres, mood, age range, and available viewing time. It explains each suggestion and offers a simple tie-breaker when the group cannot decide.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when a fictional group wants a quick, friendly way to narrow down public movie options for a casual movie night.

## Inputs

- Preferred genres or genres to avoid.
- Desired mood, such as funny, adventurous, cozy, or suspenseful.
- Maximum runtime.
- General age range or requested content rating.
- Number of suggestions to provide.

## Output

A concise shortlist of movie suggestions with the genre, approximate runtime, a one-sentence reason each fits, and one tie-breaker question. Clearly label uncertain details instead of inventing them.

## Example Prompt

```text
Plan a fictional movie night for four adults. Suggest three upbeat adventure-comedies under two hours, avoid horror, and include one tie-breaker question.
```

## Safety Notes

Use only fictional preferences and public information about movies. Do not use personal viewing histories, private account data, company information, customer data, credentials, or sensitive details. Do not claim that a movie is available on a specific streaming service unless current availability has been verified.
