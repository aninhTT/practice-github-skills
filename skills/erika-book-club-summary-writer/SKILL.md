# Erika Book Club Summary Writer

## What It Does

Turns messy, fictional book club notes into a tidy discussion recap. It groups scattered comments into themes, pulls out the big questions the group raised, and drafts a short summary the host can share before the next meeting.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when a learner wants to practice describing a note-summarizing workflow using an invented book club, without any real people, private notes, or workplace content.

## Inputs

- A fictional book title and author.
- Rough meeting notes or bullet points from the discussion.
- The names of any made-up characters or made-up attendees (optional).
- The tone you want (casual, playful, or formal).

## Output

A short recap containing a one-paragraph summary, a bulleted list of key discussion themes, and 2-3 open questions to revisit next time.

## Example Prompt

```text
Here are my rough notes from a fake book club meeting about the invented novel "The Lantern Grove." Turn them into a friendly one-paragraph recap plus a few discussion themes and open questions.
```

## Safety Notes

Use invented books, notes, and attendees only. Do not include real people's names, private messages, personal reading history, company book clubs, or any confidential or sensitive information.
