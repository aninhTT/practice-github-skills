# Weekend Hobby Picker

## What It Does

Suggests a fictional weekend hobby based on the time, weather, energy level, and budget someone provides. It gives one clear recommendation plus two backups.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when someone wants a low-stakes way to choose something fun to try on a free weekend.

## Inputs

- Available time.
- Indoor or outdoor preference.
- Desired energy level.
- Fictional budget range.
- Whether the activity should be solo or social.

## Output

- One recommended hobby with a short reason.
- A simple first step that takes less than 30 minutes.
- Two backup options.
- A brief list of any basic supplies needed.

## Example Prompt

```text
I have three free hours on Saturday, want something outdoors and moderately active,
and would rather spend less than $25. Pick a fictional hobby for me to try, give me
one easy first step, and include two backup ideas.
```

## Safety Notes

Use fictional, public-safe examples only. Do not use personal files, private history, company or customer information, internal workflows, credentials, or secrets. Avoid medical, legal, or financial guidance, and flag activities that require professional instruction or specialized safety equipment.
