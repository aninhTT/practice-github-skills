# Travel Packing List Builder

## What It Does

Takes a destination, trip length, and activity type and outputs a categorized packing checklist — clothing, toiletries, documents, electronics, and activity-specific gear — so nothing gets left behind.

## When To Use It

Use it when you're preparing for a trip and want a structured checklist tailored to your destination's climate, the length of your stay, and what you plan to do (beach vacation, business trip, hiking, city sightseeing, etc.).

## Inputs

- Destination (city and country, e.g. "Reykjavik, Iceland").
- Trip length in days (e.g. "7 days").
- Activity type (e.g. "hiking", "beach", "business", "city sightseeing").
- Optional: season or month of travel.
- Optional: carry-on only (yes/no).

## Output

A Markdown packing checklist organized into sections:

- **Clothing** (items and quantities based on trip length)
- **Toiletries**
- **Documents & Money**
- **Electronics & Accessories**
- **Activity-Specific Gear**
- **Optional / Nice to Have**

## Example Prompt

```text
Build a packing list for a 5-day hiking trip to Queenstown, New Zealand in April. Carry-on only.
```

## Safety Notes

- This is a fictional skill for GitHub practice. Do not feed it real passport numbers, booking references, or personal travel documents.
- The skill does not check visa requirements, customs rules, or airline carry-on restrictions — verify those separately.
- Gear suggestions are generic; always check destination-specific safety requirements for activities like hiking or water sports.
