# Marie Farmers Market List Builder

## What It Does

Turns a fictional weekly meal plan into a tidy farmers market shopping list. It groups items by market stall (produce, bakery, dairy, flowers), estimates rough quantities, and flags anything that might not be in season so the shopper can pick a backup.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when a learner wants to practice describing a simple planning workflow with an invented meal plan, and does not want to rely on any real personal, household, or business data.

## Inputs

- A made-up list of meals for the week.
- The number of imaginary people being cooked for.
- Any fictional dietary preferences or things to avoid.
- The made-up season or month of the market visit.

## Output

- A shopping list grouped by market stall.
- Rough quantities for each item.
- A short list of seasonal swaps in case something is unavailable.

## Example Prompt

```text
Here is a fake meal plan for a week of dinners for four people in early autumn. Build me a farmers market shopping list grouped by stall, with rough quantities and a few seasonal backup swaps.
```

## Safety Notes

Use invented meal plans and quantities only. Do not include real grocery budgets, household details, loyalty card numbers, addresses, or any real personal, customer, or company information.
