# Jasantos Karaoke Song Picker

## What It Does

Picks fictional karaoke songs for a group night out by matching songs to the group's mood, favorite decade, and how brave each singer is feeling. It suggests a short setlist, flags crowd-pleasers versus risky solo picks, and offers a backup song for anyone with stage fright.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when a learner wants to practice describing a fun group-decision workflow, like planning a fictional karaoke night, without using any real personal or workplace context.

## Inputs

- The number of singers in the fictional group.
- The group's overall mood (hype, mellow, sentimental, chaotic).
- A favorite decade or genre.
- Each singer's bravery level (shy, warmed up, fearless).

## Output

A suggested setlist of 3-5 fictional song picks with a one-line reason for each, plus one designated group sing-along closer and one backup song for shy singers.

## Example Prompt

```text
Four friends are doing a fictional karaoke night. The mood is hype, we love the 80s, and one singer is shy. Suggest a setlist with a big group closer.
```

## Safety Notes

Use invented groups and scenarios only. Do not use real names of friends or coworkers, real event details, personal history, or any company or customer information.
