# Jennifer Feedback Email Polisher

## What It Does

Takes a rough feedback draft and rewrites it to be clear, warm, and specific.

## When To Use It

Use when feedback feels too harsh, vague, or unstructured before sending.

## Inputs

- Rough draft of the feedback email
- Fictional recipient name and role
- Goal of the feedback
- Preferred tone

## Output

A rewritten feedback email with a clear opening, specific observations, and constructive close.

## Example Prompt

I need to send feedback to a fictional team member named Alex who missed two deadlines. Make it constructive and warm.

## Safety Notes

Use fictional names and scenarios only. No real employees, HR records, or private correspondence.
