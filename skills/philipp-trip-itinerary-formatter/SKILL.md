# Philipp Trip Itinerary Formatter

## What It Does

Turns a loose, unordered list of fictional travel stops into a clean day-by-day itinerary, grouping activities by day and adding simple time slots like morning, afternoon, and evening.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when a learner wants to practice describing a travel-planning workflow using a made-up trip, without relying on any real bookings, addresses, or personal travel history.

## Inputs

- A fictional destination or trip theme.
- The number of days for the trip.
- A loose list of stops, sights, or activities.
- Any pacing preference, such as relaxed or packed.

## Output

A formatted itinerary that lists each day, slots activities into morning/afternoon/evening, and ends with a short checklist of fictional things to pack.

## Example Prompt

```text
I have a fake 3-day trip to the made-up town of Brindlewick with a list of stops: lighthouse tour, market visit, hill hike, museum, and a harbor dinner. Help me turn this into a day-by-day itinerary at a relaxed pace.
```

## Safety Notes

Use invented destinations and activities only. Do not include real travel bookings, confirmation numbers, home addresses, passport or ID details, or any personal or private trip information.
