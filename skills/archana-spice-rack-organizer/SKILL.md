# Archana Spice Rack Organizer

## What It Does

Turns a rambling description of a home spice collection into an organized plan. It proposes a grouping scheme for the rack, suggests a shelf order that puts everyday jars within easy reach, and flags jars that are likely past their best based on how long ago they were opened.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when someone wants to tidy a cluttered spice rack and cannot decide whether to sort by cuisine, by how often they cook with something, or simply alphabetically. It is also useful before a big grocery run, to avoid buying a third jar of cumin.

## Inputs

- A list of spice names, however casually written.
- Roughly when each jar was opened, if known ("about a year ago" is fine).
- The rack layout: number of shelves and how many jars fit per shelf.
- A sorting preference, if the person has one: by cuisine, by frequency of use, or alphabetical.
- Any jars that must stay in a fixed spot, such as a tall grinder that only fits the bottom shelf.

## Output

- A grouping scheme with a short reason for choosing it.
- A shelf-by-shelf placement list.
- A "check these" list of jars that are probably stale, with a suggested replacement order.
- A few labeling tips so the arrangement survives the next few months of cooking.

## Example Prompt

```text
My spice rack has three shelves that hold eight jars each. I have smoked paprika,
two jars of cumin, cinnamon, turmeric, dried oregano, star anise, chili flakes,
bay leaves, and a tall pepper grinder that only fits the bottom shelf. The star
anise has been open since roughly two winters ago. I cook mostly weeknight curries
and pasta. Help me reorganize it and tell me what to toss.
```

## Safety Notes

This is a fictional practice skill. Use invented spice lists and made-up kitchens only. Do not include personal files, private notes, company or customer information, internal workflows, credentials, or anything copied from a real workplace document. Freshness suggestions are casual kitchen guesswork, not food safety guidance.
