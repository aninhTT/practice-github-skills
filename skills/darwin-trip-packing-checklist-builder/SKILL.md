# Trip Packing Checklist Builder

## What It Does

Turns a trip destination and length into a categorized packing checklist. Categories include Clothing, Toiletries, Documents, Electronics, and Gear — each tailored to the climate and activities you describe.

## When To Use It

Use it before any trip when you want a quick, organized list and don't want to think through every category from scratch. Works for weekend getaways, beach vacations, hiking trips, and city travel.

## Inputs

- Destination (city or type of place, e.g. "mountain cabin" or "tropical beach")
- Trip length in days
- Main activities (e.g. "hiking, swimming, casual dinners")
- Optional: travel season or weather ("rainy season", "winter")
- Optional: return format preference ("bullet list" or "table")

## Output

A checklist organized into these sections:

- **Clothing** (with quantities based on trip length)
- **Toiletries**
- **Documents & Cards**
- **Electronics & Accessories**
- **Gear** (activity-specific items)
- **Nice to Have** (optional extras)

## Example Prompt

```text
Build me a packing checklist.

Destination: mountain cabin
Trip length: 4 days
Activities: hiking, campfire evenings, light cooking
Season: autumn
Format: bullet list
```

## Safety Notes

- This is a fictional skill for GitHub practice. Use invented trip scenarios only.
- Do not include real personal travel plans, passport numbers, addresses, or booking details.
- The skill does not verify local entry requirements, visa rules, or health advisories — always check official sources for real travel.
