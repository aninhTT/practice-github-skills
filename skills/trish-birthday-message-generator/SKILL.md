# Birthday Message Generator

## What It Does

Writes a warm, personalized birthday message based on a few details about the recipient — their name, your relationship to them, and one or two things they're into. Adjusts tone from casual and funny to heartfelt and sincere depending on what you ask for.

## When To Use It

Use it when you want to send a thoughtful birthday message but are staring at a blank page. Works for friends, family, coworkers, or acquaintances.

## Inputs

- Recipient's first name.
- Your relationship to them (e.g. "close friend", "coworker I don't know super well", "sibling").
- One or two interests or facts about them (e.g. "loves hiking and bad puns", "just adopted a dog").
- Desired tone: casual/funny, warm/sincere, or short-and-sweet.
- Optional: a milestone to mention (e.g. "turning 30", "first birthday in a new city").

## Output

A short birthday message (2–5 sentences) ready to send as a text, card, or post. No filler phrases like "Hope your day is as special as you are."

## Example Prompt

```text
Write a birthday message for my friend Leo. He's a close friend who just got really into sourdough baking and recently moved to a new city. Tone: warm but a little funny. He's turning 30.
```

## Safety Notes

- This is a fictional skill for GitHub practice. Do not feed it real personal data, private details, or anything sensitive.
- Use invented or clearly fictional examples only.
- The skill does not know the recipient — all personalization comes from what you provide.
