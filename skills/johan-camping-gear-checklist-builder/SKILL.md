# Johan Camping Gear Checklist Builder

## What It Does

Builds a categorized packing checklist for an imaginary camping trip. It scales the list to the number of nights, the expected weather, and the size of the group, then groups items under shelter, sleep, cooking, clothing, and safety so nothing gets forgotten.

## When To Use It

Use this skill when planning a fictional camping trip and you want a tidy checklist instead of a jumbled pile of gear. It is also handy when a made-up group is splitting shared items and needs to see who is bringing the tent versus the stove.

## Inputs

- Number of nights.
- Number of people in the fictional group.
- Expected weather and rough temperature range.
- Campsite type, such as drive-in, backcountry, or lakeside.
- Any shared gear the invented group already owns.

## Output

A checklist grouped by category, with quantities scaled to the group size, a short list of shared items to divide among people, and a few notes about gear that depends on the weather.

## Example Prompt

```text
Build a camping checklist for a made-up three-night trip to a fictional
lakeside campground. Four people, cool nights around 40F, rain likely on
one day. We already have two tents and one camp stove.
```

## Safety Notes

Use invented trips, groups, and campsites only. Do not include real names, personal travel plans, private calendars, company information, customer data, or credentials. This skill produces a practice checklist for a fictional scenario and is not a substitute for real outdoor safety planning.
